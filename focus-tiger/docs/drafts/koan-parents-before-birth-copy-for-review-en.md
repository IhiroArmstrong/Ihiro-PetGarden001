## Shared rules

Optional column, first example: classic koan (wording **product-provisional · K1**). Yin is a **co-inquirer**, not a teacher. Each round: ask → sit quietly → check in → land.

- No explaining the koan, no answers, no promised outcomes.
- No religious terms in **experience-layer** speech (sources only on “About this question”).
- Every round ends with **landing** (step 5). Early exit from sitting still goes to step 5.
- Fixed script only at runtime.
- Frame as **awareness practice**, not Zen training promises.

---

## 0. First entry (firewall · once)

> This is an optional awareness practice. The question comes from a classic koan. We’ll simply sit with it for a while, together. It isn’t a religious practice, and it isn’t therapy.
>
> If you feel emotionally tight or your mind won’t stop spinning, you can skip this for now.
>
> If you feel strange, detached, or empty in an unsettling way, please stop. Don’t push through.
>
> If the word “parents” feels painful, you can skip this too.
>
> You can leave anytime. After you turn this off, we won’t bring it up again.

Buttons: **Come in** / **Not now**

## 1. The question

> Yin: Want to try a very old question together? It’s from a classic koan: Before your parents gave birth to you, what were you like?

**[K1 product-provisional]** Matches locked zh: 「父母未生你之前，你是什么样？」 Classic title on the card may still differ. Later changes: **zh + en + ja together**.

---

## 2. Invitation (no rush)

> Yin: No need to answer quickly. Close your eyes and notice how thoughts move.

---

## 3. Sit quietly (~5 minutes)

Uses existing timer. No extra on-screen explanation. Can decline or end early—no “why did you leave?” **Early end still goes to step 5 (landing).**

---

## 4. After sitting (no grading)

> Yin: What showed up just now? Thoughts, or nothing at all—both are fine. No right or wrong.

Options (descriptive, not ranked):

- Lots of thoughts
- Few thoughts
- Hard to say
- **A little uncomfortable**

- First three: Yin nods, no Confide, no generated explanation → step 5.

**Trilingual option (PO agreed 2026-11-03):** zh 有点不舒服 · en A little uncomfortable · ja 少し落ち着かない — internal reference only; not user-visible UI copy in this draft.
- **“A little uncomfortable”:** go straight to **Exit B** below (landing included).

---

## 5. Landing (every round; required after early exit)

> Yin: Let’s take three breaths and come back to right here. If focusing on breath makes you tighter, you can name three things around you, or feel your feet on the floor. Any one is enough.

Do not summarize “what you saw.”

---

## Side prompts (return visits · one line each)

| Visit | Yin says |
| --- | --- |
| 2nd | This question doesn’t seem to have an answer you can grab. Notice how the urge for an answer shows up. |
| 3rd | If your mind starts telling stories, let them run a bit—no need to do anything with them—then gently bring attention back to the question. |
| 4th | Thoughts will come. Let them, then return to the question. |
| 5th | If nothing comes up, that’s okay too. Just sitting here still counts as holding the question. |

After five rounds, no new teaching lines. Optional:

> Yin: Sit with this question once more?

**Not now** ends it. No follow-up pressure.

## Exit and landing

Landing line (same as step 5):

> Yin: Let’s take three breaths and come back to right here. If focusing on breath makes you tighter, you can name three things around you, or feel your feet on the floor. Any one is enough.

### A. “Can’t figure it out”

**Triggers (examples):** “I can’t figure it out,” “The more I think, the messier it gets.”

> Yin: Sounds like the mind is trying to grab a fixed answer. You can set that down for now, along with the question.
>
> (Landing line.)
>
> If you want to try again, you can come back on your own.

**[Product-provisional]** Interpretive line ships as written; later soften **zh + en + ja together**.

### B. “Uncomfortable”

**Triggers (examples):** “Chest tight,” “Dizzy/foggy,” or step-4 **A little uncomfortable**.

> Yin: Something feels a little off. Let’s pause. Nothing to figure out right now.
>
> (Landing line.)
>
> If you want to try again, you can come back on your own.

**[B3 locked]** zh Exit B: 「现在不用想明白什么」.

### Crisis

Self-harm, despair, etc.: stop practice immediately → existing Confide safety path. No new crisis sermons or koan “rescue.”
