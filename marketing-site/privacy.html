Privacy

Last updated: 2026-09-04

This page is a public excerpt of the Focus Tiger privacy notice. The full notice in the app may include additional sections as features evolve.

1. AI inference

AI companion features related to habits, check-ins, and practice guidance run on your device. Your related inputs are processed on your device and are not sent to a third-party large language model provider.

2. Default data storage

Unless you use local export/import, your practice records stay on your device by default. This app does not automatically upload that data to our servers.

Quiet Together / lanterns (on by default; can be turned off in Privacy): while you sit, the app sends only a random session id so a shared sit-count can stay honest. No name, email, practice log, or chat.

3. Local export / import

You may export practice data to a local JSON file and import it on the same or a new device. Exported files are not encrypted; you are responsible for keeping them safe. Import overwrites matching on-device records.

4. Cloud backup

Focus Tiger does not currently offer cloud backup through the practice app’s default path described in the full notice. Use local export/import to back up or move devices.

5. Your rights

You may delete local data anytime (in-app reset or uninstall) and export your practice backup file anytime. Questions: hello@twinsology.com
