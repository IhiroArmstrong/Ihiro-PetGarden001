Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

This page is a public excerpt of the Samatia privacy notice. The full notice in the app may include additional sections as features evolve. For wellness and non-clinical limits, see our Medical disclaimer.

1. AI inference

AI companion features related to habits, check-ins, and practice guidance run on your device. Your related inputs are processed on your device and are not sent to a third-party large language model provider.

Thought Drop (leave one passing thought in the Mac app) is open in the current release. It is not a conversation: you share one line and receive brief fixed lines—not live model generation written into a chat thread. Other on-device companion features may differ; this page describes storage and non-clinical limits for the features named below.

Thought Drop

Not a Substitute for Professional Care. Thought Drop is not a substitute for a licensed mental health professional. If you experience ongoing psychological distress or thoughts of self-harm, you should proactively seek professional help.

No Mental Health Assessment, Diagnosis, or Treatment. When you leave a passing thought, Samatia does not provide any mental health assessment, diagnosis, or treatment advice. By using Thought Drop, you acknowledge and agree to these terms.

These limits are also described in our Medical disclaimer.

2. Default data storage

Unless you use local export/import, your practice records stay on your device by default. This app does not automatically upload that data to our servers.

Quiet Together / lanterns (on by default; can be turned off in Privacy): while you sit, the app sends only a random session id so a shared sit-count can stay honest. No name, email, practice log, or chat.

Desktop update check (when idle): the Mac app may ask https://updates.twinsology.com/ whether a newer version exists. That check can show your IP and app version. It does not include practice content, chat, or your name.

3. Local export / import

You may export practice data to a local JSON file and import it on the same or a new device. Exported files are not encrypted; you are responsible for keeping them safe. Import overwrites matching on-device records.

4. Cloud backup

Samatia does not currently offer cloud backup through the practice app’s default path described in the full notice. Use local export/import to back up or move devices.

5. Your rights

You may delete local data anytime (in-app reset or uninstall) and export your practice backup file anytime. Questions: hello@twinsology.com
